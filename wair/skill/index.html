Set up your AI coach

Your coach knows the whole course. It asks one question at a time, notices patterns in your answers, and helps you draft your story in your own words. Pick the option that suits you.

Option A: the coaching skill for Claude

About 5 minutes to set up

Download the coach

Then, in Claude:

  1. Go to Settings › Capabilities and turn on code execution.
  2. Go to Customize › Skills, click +, then Create skill › Upload a skill.
  3. Upload the file you downloaded (don’t unzip it) and switch the skill on.
  4. Start a new chat and type Start Day 1

Option B: no set-up

Each day’s email links to a page with a prompt. Copy it, paste it into the AI tool you already use (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot), and the coaching starts. See the Day 1 page →

Your Brand File

At the end of each day, your coach writes a short summary of your answers. That’s your Brand File. Save it somewhere safe: a note on your phone, a document, an email to yourself. The next day, paste it in and your coach picks up where you left off.

A note on privacy

You’ll be talking about your values, your strengths and some of the moments that shaped you. Share only what you’re comfortable with. If you use AI at work, think about using a personal account for this instead.
