How to make it stick (the ETC1 system)

Under 3 minutes

ETC1 at a glance:

  1. E — Environment. Set up a trigger where you’ll see it.
  2. T — Track it all. Make your progress visible.
  3. C — Consistency beats intensity. Small and often wins.
  4. 1 — One thing at a time. Your one action. Nothing else.

Your action

Prepare your ETC1 system now, so it’s ready for your reflection session. Decide where your environmental trigger goes. Choose your tracking method. And find an accountability buddy — text someone right now and ask them to check in with you weekly. Having someone in your corner makes an enormous difference.
