What to do with the advice you get (don’t just nod and move on)

Under 3 minutes

When your responses are in, sit with them and ask yourself four questions:

  1. What surprised me? These are your blind spots.
  2. What was familiar? These are your confirmed strengths. Lock them in.
  3. What was consistent? What did more than one person say? That’s your signal.
  4. What actions can I take? One specific, observable thing. Not three. One.

Your action

Open your calendar right now. Block 1 hour, exactly 1 week from today. Call it “FAST360 Reflection.” That’s when you’ll process everything and pick your one action. Between now and then, follow up with anyone who hasn’t responded — a simple “just a friendly nudge” is all you need.
