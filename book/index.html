The story

Another star developer has just quit after a disastrous performance review. Alex Parker has had enough.

Then a chance meeting over coffee with a brilliant neuroscientist changes everything. Alex learns why the way most of us do feedback is broken — and what works instead.

FAST Forward is a short, practical story about one simple switch: stop waiting for feedback and start asking for advice. It works with the way your brain is wired, not against it.

  • Why feedback failsAnd the simple alternative that people actually want to hear.
  • The FAST frameworkFrequently Asked for, Simple and Tactical — advice you can use straight away.
  • Small changes, big resultsHow small improvements, made often, add up to real growth.
  • A culture of askingHow to build a team where people seek out advice — and act on it.
